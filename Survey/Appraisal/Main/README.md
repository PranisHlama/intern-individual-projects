# Employee Appraisals Pro

Employee Appraisals Pro is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade performance management and evaluation system for Odoo 20. It streamlines the complete employee review process—from periodic goal tracking and competency scoring to multi-source 360 feedback, review meetings, and strategic career development.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Multi-Stage Appraisal Lifecycle
* **Structured Evaluation Progression**: Pre-configured workflow stages including *Draft*, *Waiting Self-Assessment*, *Self-Assessment Submitted*, *Waiting Manager Review*, *Manager Review Completed*, *Waiting HR Validation*, *Completed*, and *Cancelled*.
* **Visual Status & Stage Gates**: Kanban and statusbar views with stage-specific validations to ensure prerequisites are satisfied before moving forward.
* **Timeline & Deadline Monitoring**: Real-time tracking of review periods, evaluation start dates, submission deadlines, and automatic overdue flagging.
* **Activity & Chatter Integration**: Native communication thread tracking stage changes, automated email reminders, and action follow-up activities.

### 2. Dedicated 360-Degree Assessment & Surveys
* **Multi-Perspective Review Workflows**:
  * **Self-Assessment**: Structured self-reflection forms with custom ratings and commentary.
  * **Peer 360 Review**: Multi-rater feedback from peers and cross-functional teammates.
  * **Manager Evaluation**: Supervisory assessment of performance metrics and competencies.
* **Decoupled Survey Architecture**: Dedicated evaluation records accessible through smart buttons and quick-generation header actions.
* **Pre-Loaded Survey Templates**: Out-of-the-box question templates for self, peer, and manager evaluations.

### 3. Competency Management & Weighted Scoring
* **Competency Framework**: Department- and role-based evaluation matrices spanning functional, behavioral, and leadership competencies.
* **Weighted Score Calculations**: Dynamic score computation combining competency weights and proficiency ratings.
* **Flexible Rating Scales**: Support for numeric scales (1–5, 1–10) and qualitative evaluation criteria.
* **Manager & HR Override**: Secure override controls for final calibration scores with mandatory justification logs.

### 4. Goal & Objective Tracking (KPIs)
* **SMART Goal Tracking**: Objective definitions with title, target deadlines, success criteria, and category tags.
* **Milestone Progress Tracking**: Real-time progress updates with synchronized completion percentage selectors (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
* **Weighted Objective Impact**: Relative goal weightings contributing to an overall composite goal score.
* **Automated Goal Carry-Forward**: Carry unfinished, in-progress goals into subsequent appraisal cycles with full lineage tracking.

### 5. 1-on-1 Performance Meetings & Scheduling
* **Calendar Integration**: Direct scheduling of appraisal discussion meetings linked to Odoo Calendar.
* **Virtual Meeting Support**: Direct video conference link generation and tracking.
* **Meeting Documentation**: Dedicated capture of key discussion points, commitments, and agreed action items.

### 6. Career Development & Skill Gap Analysis
* **Skill Matrix Alignment**: Gap analysis comparing target job competencies with current employee proficiency levels.
* **Career Development Planning**: Actionable development plans with milestone dates and review tracking.
* **Training & Mentorship Recommendations**: Targeted training course assignments and mentoring programs.

### 7. Automated Recurring Appraisal Plans
* **Automated Batch Scheduling**: Rule-based generation of appraisal cycles across monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual intervals.
* **Department & Role Filtering**: Tailored appraisal plan assignments for specific departments or job positions.
* **Automated Background Reminders**: System cron jobs that track upcoming deadlines, issue reminders, and escalate overdue appraisals.

### 8. Batch Wizards & Administrative Utilities
* **Batch Appraisal Generation**: Launch reviews across entire departments or company-wide in a single operation.
* **Bulk Manager Reassignment**: Efficiently transfer pending appraisals when reporting lines or team structures change.
* **Bulk Deadline Extension**: Update review deadlines in batch with recorded audit reasons.
* **Targeted Notification Dispatcher**: Send immediate reminder notifications to appraisers or employees.
* **Appraisal Data Export**: Export structured performance data and scoring summaries.

### 9. Reporting, Analytics & Executive Dashboards
* **Multi-Dimensional Pivot Analysis**: Group and aggregate performance scores by department, manager, stage, and period.
* **Graphical Distributions**: Bar and line charts visualizing score spreads, stage progression, and completion rates.
* **Executive PDF Reports**: Clean, printable appraisal summaries including competency ratings, goal progress, and final sign-offs.

### 10. Role-Based Security & Confidentiality
* **Granular Access Control**: Dedicated permission groups for *Employee*, *Manager*, *HR Officer*, and *HR Administrator*.
* **Confidential Note Isolation**: Private evaluation notes and HR calibration feedback visible only to authorized roles.
* **Multi-Company Architecture**: Complete data scoping and company isolation for multi-entity deployments.
